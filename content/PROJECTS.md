## Stages

- `drafting` — content being written or structured
- `reviewing` — draft under review or revision
- `ready` — approved, ready to publish
- `published` — content has been published (archive soon)
- `paused` — work paused, waiting on external input

## Archive Reasons

- `published` — content was published
- `abandoned` — project dropped, no longer relevant
- `inactive` — no activity for 30+ days, no recovery plan
- `merged` — absorbed into another content project
